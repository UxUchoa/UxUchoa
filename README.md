Today I work as a **Software Engineer focused on data analysis**: turning product and operational data into dashboards, metrics and decisions.

I started out writing code, spent years designing interfaces and researching users, and now I live right in the middle: **I understand the product problem, design the experience and ship it**. About 6 years across UX/UI, research, data and development, always asking the same thing: *does this actually solve the problem for the person using it?*

My process fits in three verbs: **observe → structure → build.**

- 🔍 **Product & research:** discovery, interviews, usability testing, journeys, NPS/CES and evidence-based prioritization.
- 📊 **Data:** SQL, Python, dashboards and product metrics that turn numbers into decisions.
- 🛠️ **Fullstack:** React, TypeScript, Next.js, Python/FastAPI, APIs, databases and automation.
- 🧠 **Local AI & privacy:** I like *local-first* software, with models running on your own machine through Ollama and data that never leaves without you knowing.
